# alist 阿里云盘open

本文源自网络，转载请标明出处。

原文链接：https://www.cnblogs.com/anyview/p/17357064.html

1、首先，要确保你的Alist版本为v3.12.0（或者以上的版本）；

[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326084-1-20241005162604789.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326084-1.jpg)

 

 

2、在电脑的浏览器打开【[这个地址](https://alist.nn.ci/tool/aliyundrive/request)】，如果你要输入账号密码那就点“Go to login”，我这里选择用手机APP扫码所以点“Scan QrCode”（这两种方式都可以，根据自己操作习惯选择）；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326086-2-20241005162609902.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326086-2.jpg)

 

 

3、网页会显示一个二维码，打开手机上的阿里云盘APP，扫码；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326087-3-20241005162613478.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326087-3.jpg)

 

 

4、在手机APP弹出的“Alist申请权限”对话框里点“同意”；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326088-4-20241005162618043.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326088-4.jpg)

 

 

5、在电脑浏览器上点“I have scan”；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326090-5-20241005162624695.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326090-5.jpg)

 

 

6、网页上显示有“refresh_token”的内容，选中全部的内容，复制；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326091-6-20241005162628283.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326091-6.jpg)

 

 

7、在Alist后台管理的菜单，存储，添加；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326093-7-20241005162634332.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326093-7.jpg)

 

 

8、类型选“阿里云盘开放平台”；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326095-8-20241005162639301.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326095-8.jpg)

 

 

9、挂载路径选择根目录填写/[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326099-9-20241005162642290.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326099-9.jpg)

 

 

10、在刷新令牌处，把第6个步骤复制的“refresh_token”的全部内容，粘贴进去，添加；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326100-10-20241005162645141.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326100-10.jpg)

 

 

11、此时后台已经能看到添加好的阿里云盘开放平台了；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326102-11-20241005162648010.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326102-11.jpg)

 

 

12、打开Alist，前端已经可以看到刚刚添加的阿里云盘；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326103-12-20241005162650566.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326103-12.jpg)

 

 

13、点进去，就可以看到你挂载的阿里云盘里面的内容；[![img](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zz3656/picgo@main/img/1677326104-13-20241005162655163.jpg)](https://wp.gxnas.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1677326104-13.jpg)

 

 

14、找个视频，测试一下播放，没有问题，拖动也没有问题；
